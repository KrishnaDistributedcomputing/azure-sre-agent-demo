How to use this dashboard

A guide for business, operations, and engineering audiences
1

Start with the executive summary

Use the headline, coverage, and impact mix to explain whether the demonstration has enough evidence to support a reliability conversation.

2

Choose a business risk

Select any scenario card. Start with business impact and recovery, then move into technical evidence and investigation when more detail is needed.

3

Validate the evidence

Use the trend and evidence table to connect the narrative to timestamps, status codes, duration, root cause, and correlation IDs.

4

Discuss the SRE Agent role

Explain how the agent helps reduce investigation time by correlating telemetry and proposing a likely fault domain. Human approval remains part of remediation.

5

Confirm cost controls

Review the budget, ingestion limit, active-flow allocation, scale-to-zero hosting, and teardown date before running additional demonstrations.

6

Define a decision

Finish each walkthrough with a recovery criterion, an owner, and a prevention action that can be measured in the next exercise.
